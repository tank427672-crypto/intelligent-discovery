## 中文发布帖

Intelligent Discovery / 智能发现现已开源。

我们在做的不是一个替人下结论的 AI，而是一个帮助人们发现世界变化、理解案例经验、查看证据和限制、提出更好问题的知识发现系统。

它的核心原则很简单：重要结论要能追溯到来源与证据；未知项必须保留；AI 可以整理、提示和解释，但不能代替人的判断。

当前项目已经完成发现任务、来源与证据链、案例资产、知识关系、反馈与治理、世界发现候选、内容探索和生态扩展边界等工程基础。它仍处于 Web Beta 候选阶段：没有开放生产账户、自动联网发现、公开社区或黑箱推荐。我们会清楚区分已实现能力、候选内容和未来规划。

如果你关心可信 AI、知识管理、开源协作、案例学习、趋势理解或决策辅助，欢迎阅读项目、提出问题、补充来源、提交案例线索或参与代码贡献。

项目地址：`https://github.com/tank427672-crypto/intelligent-discovery`

## English announcement

Intelligent Discovery is now open source.

We are not building an AI that makes decisions for people. We are building an evidence-led discovery system that helps people understand world changes, learn from cases, inspect sources and limitations, and ask better questions.

Our principles are straightforward: important claims should be traceable to sources and evidence; uncertainty must remain visible; AI may organize and explain, but people retain judgment and control.

The project is currently a Web Beta candidate. Its engineering foundation includes discovery tasks, evidence chains, case intelligence, knowledge relations, feedback and governance, world-event candidates, and discovery experience contracts. It does not yet offer production accounts, autonomous web acquisition, an open community, or black-box recommendations.

Contributors interested in trustworthy AI, knowledge systems, open-source collaboration, case learning, trend interpretation, or decision support are welcome.

Repository: `https://github.com/tank427672-crypto/intelligent-discovery`

## 传播建议

- 先面向可信 AI、知识管理、开源 Python、研究方法和产品治理社区，避免夸大“通用智能”叙事。

- 先邀请少量能提供高质量反馈的测试者，而不是追求大量注册或浏览量。
